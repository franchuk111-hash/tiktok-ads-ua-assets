BEFORE YOU PRESS CREATE ACCOUNT

Three decisions that
make the first spend count.

You do not need a finished campaign to register. You do need a clear idea of what the account is for.

Minimum daily budgets, available objectives and ad placements are set by TikTok and differ by objective, country and account history — check the current values inside Ads Manager before you commit a budget.

01
◎

Decide who the account is for

TikTok’s registration asks for business details, so decide up front whether the account stands for a brand, an online store or an agency client. One account per business keeps billing and reporting readable.

02
✦

Read the credit tiers carefully

The new advertiser offer is tiered: a higher qualifying spend unlocks a larger credit. Tiers are not guaranteed and the offer shown inside TikTok’s registration flow, with its terms, controls what you actually get.

03
◉

Pick the signal you will trust

Choose the number that means success before you spend — a purchase, a lead, an app install or a cost per thousand views. Ads Manager reporting and your tracking setup are where that number comes from.

GOOD TO KNOW

Before you
get started.

Here are a few things to know about creating a TikTok Ads account.

Where do I register for a TikTok Ads account?+

Use the button on this page to continue to TikTok’s registration flow. Account registration takes place on TikTok’s website.

What information will I need?+

TikTok will guide you through the information needed for your account. Requirements may vary depending on your country and business type.

How much does it cost to run ads?+

Campaign costs depend on the settings you choose and the options available to your account. Review current budget and billing details in TikTok Ads Manager before launching a campaign.

Is there a minimum budget for TikTok ads?+

TikTok sets minimum daily budgets and they differ by objective, country and billing currency. The exact figure is shown inside Ads Manager when you set up a campaign, so check it there rather than relying on an older guide.

What kind of ad creative does TikTok Ads use?+

TikTok ads are built around vertical full-screen video: short clips that feel native to the feed rather than polished television spots. Ads Manager lists the supported formats, placements and current creative specifications for your objective.

How do I measure results from a campaign?+

Ads Manager reports delivery and engagement metrics such as impressions, clicks, reach and cost per thousand views. Conversions, purchases or app installs need a tracking setup such as the TikTok Pixel or an app SDK before data appears.

Are the new advertiser ad credits guaranteed?+

No. The spend tiers shown here are a guide to the incentive details supplied for this page. TikTok’s offer shown during registration and its terms control. Eligibility, qualifying spend windows, availability, and reward timing may vary by advertiser and region.

Is this an official TikTok website?+

No. This is an independent informational page. TikTok Ads accounts, approval, features, and availability are managed by TikTok and may vary by country.
